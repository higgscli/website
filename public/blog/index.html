    Jev decision models: sharper classification, still on your hardware

    higgs classify can now use a local Jev decision model. On 155 real emails, Open-Jev-2B scored an AUROC of 0.87 against 0.78 for gemma4:e4b and caught 29 of 35 needs-attention emails where Gemma caught 24, at about the same false-alarm level. We cover the benchmark, the hardware caveats, and the setup.
